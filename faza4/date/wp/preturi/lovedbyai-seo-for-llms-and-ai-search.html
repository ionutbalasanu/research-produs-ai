Safe for your WordPress site

  • Your design, content and speed stay the same

  • No code, no developer, no page edits

  • Fully reversible: deactivate and it's gone

Safe for your SEO too

Think of it like the common practice of running a landing page for each ad: the same business, a version made for the reader. Google keeps ranking the page you already have.

  • Your pages stay the canonical ones Google indexes
  • Search bots aren’t redirected to a different page
  • Doesn’t override your existing SEO work
  • Adds the schema and meta data Google reads too

What the plugin doesn't do, on purpose

  • No blog spamWe don't write or publish articles for you. Your content stays yours.
  • No rip-and-replaceIt works alongside your SEO plugin and your agency, not instead of them.
  • No traffic promisesNobody can promise AI traffic. Your dashboard shows what actually changes.
