How does the seven-day trial work?

Choose a paid plan and start the free trial. You pay nothing for 7 days. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your selected monthly or annual price is charged automatically. One free trial is available per account.

Can I just stay on Free?

Yes. Free is a permanent plan with one domain, three widgets, up to ten reviews and monthly syncing. GR Widget branding (not shown on WordPress plugin installs) remains on web embeds. No payment details are needed.

What counts as a website?

Each domain is one website address. Paid plans let you place unlimited widgets across pages on the domains included in your plan. Free includes three widgets on one domain.

What counts as a Google business?

One Google Business Profile, matched by its Place ID. Several widgets on the same listing count as one Google business. Each plan includes the same number of Google businesses as websites.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade from Account & billing. Paid-plan changes are confirmed before a downgrade. Changing plans does not provide another free trial.

Are views limited on paid plans?

No. All paid plans include unlimited views and unlimited widgets. The number of websites, Google businesses, and reviews follows the plan you choose.

Do I need to connect my Google account?

No. Find your public Google business listing by name. You don’t need to sign in with Google or connect your Business Profile.
