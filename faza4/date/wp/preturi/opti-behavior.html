Pricing FAQ

Questions agencies and site owners ask before choosing.

Do you charge by session, pageview, or recording?
No. Opti-Behavior is self-hosted, so your pricing is based on the plan and site count, not visitor volume. Paid plans have no monthly session or recording limit. Pro Free, after the trial, records up to 1,000 sessions per month.
Can I use Free without paying?
Yes. Free includes the core dashboard, click heatmaps, funnels, A/B testing, Smart Insights core recommendations, email reports, and unlimited sessions.
What happens after the Pro trial?
The trial lasts 6 months and starts from the free plugin, no card needed. When it ends, your site keeps Pro for free on 1 site (Pro Free): every Pro feature keeps working, and up to 1,000 new sessions are recorded each month. The counter resets on the 1st of each month; above it, new recordings pause until the next month. Recordings already made stay on your server. A paid Pro license removes this monthly limit. You can buy Pro at €89/year and enter your license key in Opti-Behavior → Settings → License & Quota, or continue using Free. Step-by-step guide: when your Pro trial ends.
Are Business and Agency yearly renewals?
Yes. They are annual licenses for 5 and 10 sites. Early customers get the founding annual price.
Can I use this for client sites?
Yes. Business and Agency are designed for freelancers and agencies managing client analytics while keeping each client’s behavior data self-hosted.
Do I need to share visitor data with OptiUser?
No. Analytics data stays on your WordPress server. Support may ask for diagnostics only if you choose to share them.
