Questions

Do I need a Trustpilot account?

Yes. You need a Trustpilot business profile with reviews. Paste its link into Reviewkit and you are connected.

Do I need an API key or developer?

No. Setup is pasting your Trustpilot link and clicking Sync Reviews.

Will it slow down my site?

No. Reviews are stored on your site and styles load only on pages that show a widget.

Can I try Pro before buying?

Start with the free plugin, which already shows your reviews. Add Pro when you want more control. Refunds follow the policy shown at checkout.

What happens when my license ends?

Your reviews keep displaying with the free plugin. Pro features and updates pause until you renew.

Is Reviewkit affiliated with Trustpilot?

No. Reviewkit is independent and only shows publicly available review data.
