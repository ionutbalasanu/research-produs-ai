A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

More in the docs
Do I need Elementor or another page builder?

No. Blockenberg works in the native WordPress Block Editor, also known as Gutenberg. You don’t need Elementor to edit your pages.

Can I edit the imported templates?

Yes. Templates import as editable Gutenberg blocks. Change the text, images, colors and layout in the block editor, rather than editing one large HTML block.

What does Pro add?

Pro unlocks the full template library: 683 page templates across 76 collections. Keep Blockenberg Free active alongside the Pro plugin.

Is $6 a monthly or yearly price?

Neither. It is a one-time payment in US dollars for a lifetime license, with use on unlimited websites. The checkout shows your final total.

Is AI usage included in the Pro price?

The AI Agent is available in the free plugin. You connect your own provider through OpenRouter, including free models, or use a local Codex, Claude or Cursor session. Model usage depends on the provider you choose.

Where can I get help?

Start with the documentation, or visit the Blockenberg support forum on WordPress.org.
