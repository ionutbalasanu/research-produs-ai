FREE ADDONS FOR ELEMENTOR

Two focused plugins that fill the gaps in free Elementor.

Lightweight, open source, and built to feel like part of Elementor.

THE PLUGINS

Install one, or both. They work together.

Better Post & Filter Widgets for Elementor

POST, FILTERING, SEARCH

AJAX filtering, search and sorting for posts, products, users and terms, plus a flexible post widget with grids, carousels and loop templates.

Coming in 2.0.0: filter users and taxonomies, for member directories, team pages and category grids.

Get it on WordPress.org

Architect - Complete Theme Builder for Elementor

THEME BUILDER

Headers, footers, single and archive templates and popups for Elementor, with display conditions to choose where each one appears.

Built with it: this site’s header, footer and documentation pages are Architect templates.

Get it on WordPress.org
